How to compress a photo to 20 KB or 50 KB for online forms

By Anand Kumar · Updated 28 October 2026

Free, private, and nothing is uploaded.

Open Compress Image

Most online application forms (for exams, jobs, scholarships and admissions) set a maximum file size for your photo and signature, often somewhere between 10 KB and 100 KB. A normal phone photo is 2–5 MB, so the form rejects it. Here's how to bring it under the limit in a few seconds.

Before you start: check the form's rules

Open the official notification or the upload page and note down:

  • File size: the maximum, and sometimes a minimum, for example "10 KB to 50 KB".
  • Dimensions: in pixels (like 200 × 230 px) or centimetres (like 3.5 × 4.5 cm).
  • Format: usually JPG or JPEG.
  • Background and other rules: for example a light or white background, or a recent photo.

Always follow the official notification. Limits are different for every exam and can change from year to year.

Steps

  1. Open the Compress Image tool on SaralPDF.
  2. Add your photo. Drag it in, tap Choose files, or paste a screenshot with Ctrl + V.
  3. Under Compress by, choose Max file size.
  4. Type the limit from the form, or tap a quick option such as 20 KB or 50 KB.
  5. Set Save as to JPG, which almost every form accepts.
  6. Tap Compress images. You'll see the size before and after, for example "3.2 MB → 48 KB".
  7. Tap Save and upload the new file to the form.

Shortcut: the Exam Photo & Signature tool does cropping, exact pixel size and the KB range in one step, with presets for IBPS, SSC, NEET and UPSC.

If the form also asks for exact dimensions

Resize first, then compress. Use the Resize Image tool to set the width and height in pixels, then compress the resized photo. If the form gives sizes in cm, our guide on converting cm to pixels shows how.

Doing a signature

  1. Sign with a black or dark blue pen on plain white paper.
  2. Photograph it in daylight, holding the phone straight above the paper.
  3. Crop away the extra paper with Crop Image (choose Signature 2:1).
  4. Clean it up with Enhance Quality: choose Document and Black and white.
  5. Compress it to the required size, often 10–20 KB.

Tips for a clear result

  • Start from a sharp, well-lit photo. Compression cannot fix blur.
  • If the form has a minimum size and your file is too small, set a higher limit, for example 45 KB when the range is 20–50 KB.
  • If SaralPDF shows "Scaled to … px to fit the size limit", the photo was made smaller to fit. If the form needs exact dimensions, resize to those dimensions first.

Frequently asked questions

Does compressing reduce photo quality?

A little. Smaller files keep fewer details, but at 20–100 KB a passport-style photo still looks clear on a form. Start from a sharp original for the best result.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. SaralPDF compresses the photo inside your browser. It never leaves your phone or computer.

Can I compress many photos at once?

Yes. Add several photos and they are all compressed to the same limit. Save them one by one or all together as a ZIP file.
