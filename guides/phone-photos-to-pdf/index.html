How to turn phone photos of documents into a clean PDF

By Anand Kumar · Updated 28 October 2026

Free, private, and nothing is uploaded.

Open Image to PDF

No scanner? Your phone camera is enough. With a little cleanup, photos of documents can look almost like proper scans.

1. Take good photos

  • Place the document on a flat, dark surface so the edges stand out.
  • Use daylight near a window, and avoid your own shadow falling on the page.
  • Hold the phone straight above the page, not at an angle.
  • Fill the frame with the page, and crop any extra in your gallery app.

2. Clean them up

  1. Open Enhance Quality and add all the photos.
  2. Set This is a to Document.
  3. Choose Grayscale for most pages, Black and white for the sharpest text, or Keep colour if there are stamps or coloured signatures.
  4. Tap Enhance and save the cleaned images (Save all as ZIP is handy).

3. Make the PDF

  1. Open Image to PDF and add the cleaned images.
  2. Arrange the pages in order by dragging, or with the arrows.
  3. Choose A4, orientation Auto and margin Small.
  4. Name the file and tap Create PDF, then Save.

Shortcut: you can also make the PDF first and then put the PDF itself through Enhance Quality. It cleans every page in one go.

4. Check the size

If the form has an upload limit, compress the PDF with Compress PDF. Black-and-white pages compress the most.

Frequently asked questions

Will Enhance Quality fix a blurry photo?

It improves contrast and sharpness, but it can't recover detail the camera never captured. Retake the photo if the text is unreadable.

Which is better, grayscale or black and white?

Black and white gives the cleanest text and smallest files. Grayscale keeps soft details like photos and light stamps.
