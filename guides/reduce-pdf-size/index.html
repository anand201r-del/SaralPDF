How to reduce PDF size for upload

By Anand Kumar · Updated 30 October 2026

Free, private, and nothing is uploaded.

Open Compress PDF

Upload portals often limit PDFs to 1 MB, 500 KB or even 200 KB. Scanned documents and PDFs made from phone photos are usually much bigger. Here's how to shrink them.

Why scanned PDFs are so big

Each page of a scanned PDF is a large, high-resolution image. Ten pages photographed on a phone can easily reach 20–30 MB. Lowering the resolution and compressing those page images makes the file much smaller while keeping it readable.

Steps

  1. Open the Compress PDF tool and add your PDF.
  2. Under Keep each PDF under, type the limit from your form, or tap a size such as 200 KB, 300 KB or 1 MB.
  3. For scanned text documents, tick Convert to black and white. More of the size goes to sharp text, so small limits look better.
  4. Tap Compress PDFs. SaralPDF tries different resolutions and qualities and keeps the sharpest version that fits. Look for the green Fits under badge.
  5. Prefer to choose the quality yourself? Switch Compress by to Level and pick Light, Recommended or Strong.
  6. Open the result and zoom in to make sure the text is still easy to read, then save.

Which size should I choose?

Target sizeGood for
100 KB · 200 KBStrict portals, 1–3 page certificates. Tick black and white.
300 KB · 500 KBMost exam and job forms, marksheets
1 MB · 2 MBLong documents, email attachments

Good to know

  • Compress PDF saves each page as an image, so text in the result can't be selected or searched. That's fine for scans and uploads.
  • PDFs created directly from Word or other software are usually small already. If compressing would make the file bigger, SaralPDF keeps your original.
  • If you're merging several documents, merge first and compress once at the end.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Compression happens on your device. Your document is never sent anywhere.

Can I compress password-protected PDFs?

Not directly. Open it with its password in a PDF reader, save a copy without the password, then compress.
