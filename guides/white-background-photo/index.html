How to make a photo background white

By Anand Kumar · Updated 24 October 2026

Free, private, and nothing is uploaded.

Open Remove Background

Many forms and ID cards want a photo with a plain white or light background. If your photo was taken at home, you can replace the background in a few seconds.

Steps

  1. Open the Remove Background tool and add your photo.
  2. Under New background, choose White. Use Colour if your form asks for light blue or another colour.
  3. Choose the AI model. Fast works well for most photos, and Best handles hair edges better.
  4. Tap Remove background. The first time, the AI model downloads to your browser (about 40 MB, or 80 MB for Best). After that it's quick.
  5. Check the preview, especially around the hair and shoulders, then tap Save.

Then size it for the form

  1. Set the exact dimensions with Resize Image.
  2. Bring it under the KB limit with Compress Image.

Tips for the best result

  • Stand in front of a plain wall, away from clutter, with light falling on your face.
  • Wear clothes that contrast with the wall. A white shirt against a white wall makes edges harder for the AI.
  • Use a sharp, front-facing photo. The AI can't fix blur.

Follow the form's rules. Some applications require a photo taken against a light background and don't allow edited photos. Changing the background must never change how you look.

Frequently asked questions

Is my photo uploaded to remove the background?

No. Only the AI model is downloaded to your browser. Your photo is processed on your device and stays there.

Why is it slow the first time?

The AI model downloads once (about 40–80 MB). Your browser keeps it, so later uses are much faster.

Can I get a transparent background?

Yes. Choose Transparent and the result is saved as a PNG.
