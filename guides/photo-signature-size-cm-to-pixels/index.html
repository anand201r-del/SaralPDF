Photo and signature size: converting cm to pixels

By Anand Kumar · Updated 28 October 2026

Free, private, and nothing is uploaded.

Open Resize Image

Some forms describe the photo size in centimetres, like 3.5 × 4.5 cm, while photo tools work in pixels. Here's how to convert between them and resize your photo correctly.

The formula

Pixels depend on resolution, measured in DPI (dots per inch). One inch is 2.54 cm.

pixels = cm ÷ 2.54 × DPI

Example: a width of 3.5 cm at 200 DPI is 3.5 ÷ 2.54 × 200 = 276 pixels.

If the form doesn't mention DPI, 200 or 300 DPI is a common choice. If the form states the size directly in pixels, use those numbers and skip the conversion.

Resize your photo

  1. First crop the photo to the right shape with Crop Image, for example Passport photo 3.5 × 4.5 cm.
  2. Open the Resize Image tool and add your photo.
  3. Under Unit, choose cm and type the size from your form, for example 3.5 × 4.5. Set DPI to the value your form asks for (usually 200 or 300). SaralPDF shows the pixel size it will make. You can also choose Pixels and type them directly.
  4. For an exact size, untick Keep aspect ratio. If your crop was close to the right shape, the photo won't look stretched.
  5. Set Save as to JPG and tap Resize images.
  6. If the form also has a KB limit, open Compress Image next and compress the resized photo.

Quick check: will it look stretched?

Divide width by height for both your photo and the target size. If the two numbers are close (for example 0.78 and 0.80), resizing to the exact size will look natural. If they're far apart, crop the photo again first.

Frequently asked questions

What DPI should I use?

Use the DPI the form mentions. If it doesn't mention one, the pixel size usually matters more than DPI. 200 or 300 DPI are common choices.

Why does my resized photo look stretched?

The original shape didn't match the target shape. Crop the photo closer to the target shape first, then resize.
