Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

SaralPDF is built so that your documents stay with you. This page explains what happens to your files and what information, if any, is collected when you use saralpdf.in.

Your files never leave your device

Every tool on SaralPDF, including Image to PDF, Merge PDF, Compress PDF, Compress Image, Crop Image, Exam Photo & Signature, Convert Image, Enhance Quality and Remove Background, runs inside your web browser. The files you choose are read and processed on your own phone or computer. They are not uploaded to SaralPDF or to any other server, and we cannot see, store or share them.

When you close or refresh the page, the files and results are cleared from the page. Files you save are stored only where you save them.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to sign up or log in.
  • We do not collect your name, phone number or address.
  • SaralPDF does not currently use analytics or tracking cookies.

Services that load when you visit

To work, the site loads some files from trusted third-party services. Like any website request, these services can see technical details such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request. They do not receive your documents.

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website files.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts used on the site.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) and jsDelivr provide the open-source libraries that power the tools (pdf-lib, PDF.js, JSZip, heic2any).
  • IMG.LY provides the AI model for Remove Background. The model is downloaded to your browser the first time you use that tool. Your photos are processed on your device and are not sent to IMG.LY.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Photo location data

Photos taken on phones often contain hidden information (EXIF data), such as the date, camera model and GPS location. When SaralPDF compresses, resizes or enhances an image, the new file is created from the image pixels only, so this hidden information is not copied into the result.

Advertising

SaralPDF may show advertisements in the future to keep the tools free. If ads are added, they may be provided by Google AdSense. In that case:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.
  • You can learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Advertising never gives anyone access to the files you process with SaralPDF, because those files never leave your device.

Emails you send us

If you email us for feedback or support, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you and improve SaralPDF, and we do not sell or share it.

Children

SaralPDF is a general-purpose tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example when advertising or analytics are added, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email our contact address or visit the Contact page.
