      How to use it

      1. Choose your exam and whether it's a photo or signature.
      2. Add your photo or a picture of your signature.
      3. Move the box over your face or signature.
      4. Tap Make exam file. You get the exact size and KB range.

      Questions

      Which exams are included?

      IBPS and bank exams, SSC signature, NEET and UPSC, plus passport size and a custom size you can set yourself.

      Why is there no SSC photo preset?

      SSC now captures your photo live during the online application, so only the signature needs to be uploaded.

      What if my form's size is different?

      Edit the width, height and KB values, or pick Custom size. Always follow your official notification.
