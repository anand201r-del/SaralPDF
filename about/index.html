About SaralPDF

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Saral means simple. SaralPDF is a free set of everyday document tools for students, job seekers and anyone filling in online forms.

Why SaralPDF exists

Online forms for exams, jobs and scholarships ask for very specific files: a photo under 50 KB, a signature of an exact size, all certificates in one PDF. Many free tools upload your private documents to their servers, show heavy ads, or ask you to sign up. SaralPDF does these jobs simply, and your files never leave your device.

What you can do

  • Image to PDF: turn photos and scans into one PDF.
  • PDF to Image: save PDF pages as JPG or PNG.
  • Merge PDF: join several PDFs in the order you choose.
  • Compress PDF: make scanned PDFs small enough to upload.
  • Compress Image: reduce a photo to an exact size such as 20 KB or 50 KB.
  • Resize Image: change dimensions in pixels or by percentage.
  • Enhance Quality: clean up dark, shadowy phone scans.
  • Remove Background: get a transparent or white background with on-device AI.

Private by design

Every tool runs in your browser using open-source libraries (pdf-lib, PDF.js, JSZip and @imgly/background-removal). Nothing is uploaded. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Who made it

SaralPDF is built by Anand Kumar, an M.Tech (Computer Science and Engineering) student at IIIT Guwahati. The source code is open on GitHub.
