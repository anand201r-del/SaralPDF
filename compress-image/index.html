      How to use it

      1. Add your photos.
      2. Choose Max file size and enter the KB limit, or tap 20 KB or 50 KB.
      3. Pick JPG as the format for forms.
      4. Tap Compress images and save.

      Questions

      How do I compress a photo to 20 KB?

      Choose Max file size, tap 20 KB and press Compress images. SaralPDF finds the best quality that fits under 20 KB.

      Does compression remove location data?

      Yes. Re-saved photos don't keep hidden GPS or camera data.
